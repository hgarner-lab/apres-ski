How to play McCURLING

You take the first shot. Then the system takes its turn. You each get three stones, and you can bump the other side’s stones out of the way. Each stone of yours closer to the centre than the system’s closest stone wins you a free shot at the party. If you end up with one of yours on the centre spot, you double your free party shots!

Game controls:

  1. Choose your line by dragging the line, or using the aim buttons, or the left and right arrows.
  2. Build your power by holding the up arrow, or by pressing the 'Hold to push' button.
  3. Then release to make your stone slide.
